## Round 20 checkpoint and direction

**Proved by the present fresh-context analytic audit:** the RORR baseline decomposition, all-phase visit bound and both entrance cancellations justify the round-19 bound in its exact supplied even-n,T=2 model. See [audit.md](audit.md), [memory.md](memory.md), [recovery.json](recovery.json) and [verification.json](verification.json). The recovered WIP's missing completion receipts are not retroactively asserted to exist.

**Unchanged empirical status:** the original n=256 benefit and separately registered n=512 benefit have finite empirical support. The original capped high-noise main trial, 240 missing gate-blocked holdout rows and inconclusive original two-stage decision remain intact. This round does not resample, redo every receipt analysis, or certify absent private logs. See [round 3](../round-0003/report.md).

**Unresolved in the project:** complete fourth-order gap, useful uniform remainder, fixed-c ordering and transfer to ordinary initialization. Earlier positive-majorant counterexamples remain counterexamples to those proof methods. No current literature search was performed; novelty is **UNVERIFIED**.

**Next lane: larger T.** The T=2 word audit is now bounded and complete. A distinct useful question is to expose the T-dependence of the round-5 donor/forest estimate for T=T(n), especially T≈log n and T≈√n: does its exponential synchronization factor represent only a proof loss? This can start with analytic inequalities and deterministic graph checks; no new Monte Carlo study is registered here.
