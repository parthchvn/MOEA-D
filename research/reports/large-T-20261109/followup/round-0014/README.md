# Round 14: independent cubic proof audit

**Proved under the supplied pinned-model assumptions:** the round-13
candidate bound |C_n|<=10^10 n^7 survives independent audit. The
[analytic argument](audit.md) retains both acceptance factors, defines the
orphan hitting function at every phase, and bounds all six mixed cubic words.
C_n is the p^3 coefficient of the T=3 minus T=2 boundary completion-time gap
in sweeps; attempts multiply it by n+1. This is not a uniform Taylor
remainder or a result for ordinary initialization.

These are signed T=2 coefficient components, not trajectory probabilities or
the T=3 minus T=2 gap. All 16 words, including RRR and OOO, match the published
exact rational hashes. The independent full sum matches each published T=2
cubic. Unit checks also reproduce the two n=3 arm cubics, which handle the
finite exceptional case in the all-n proof.

**Contradicted shortcut:** using a boundary hitting function unchanged at an
interior phase gives a negative supposed excess. At n=4, orphan weight zero,
all nonorphans correct and next breeder 1, the phase mean is 255.8 sweeps
but the boundary mean is 256. Their difference is -1/5. The corrected
phase-to-phase difference is zero. The earlier code already subtracted
phase values; this audit supplies the explicit definition and proof.

**Mechanically checked scope:**

- 238,166 two-birth support histories at n=3,...,8, including 16,376 bad-pair
  histories; none loses either required acceptance factor. This exhausts a
  superset of relevant clean-descendant histories, not arbitrary noisy paths.
- 19,166 rational acceptance inequalities at n=3,...,16.
- 456 states at n=4,6, including 100 bad-pair states and the orphan-fed last
  pair: 1,368 stopped-reward identities, 8,856 local phase identities, 8,856
  annihilation identities, 11,808 excess upper/nonnegativity checks, the
  additional predictable-count bounds, and 4,452 R-response/forcing bounds.
- The largest checked ratio to the predictable-count bound is 0.058004;
  the largest R-response ratio is 0.00009625. The constants are deliberately
  loose. Finite ratios are not evidence of a sharp asymptotic rate.

All new calculations are deterministic exact arithmetic. There are no new
scientific trajectories, seeds, sampling intervals, fitted laws or censoring.

**Empirically supported, unchanged:** the original n=256 T=1/T=16 ratio is
2.327639 [2.050148,2.674642], with 24 complete observations per arm. The
separately registered n=512 T=1/T=23 restricted-mean ratio is 2.339095
[2.085387,2.596468], with 32 complete observations per arm. These are the
previous approximate nominal 95% bootstrap intervals, not new estimates.
[dependency.json](dependency.json) retains distributions, phase denominators,
seed separation and censoring. The independent follow-up does not reopen
the original holdout: its 240 trials remain missing and gate-blocked, and
the original two-stage decision remains inconclusive. No plan or receipt
repair was needed. Private earlier trial logs remain unavailable, limiting
chronology verification to the published evidence.

**Unresolved:** fourth-and-higher terms uniformly in n, a sharp cubic order
or sign, fixed-positive-c ordering at p=c/n, and ordinary-initialization
behavior. No enlarged noise window follows from this cubic theorem alone.
The next useful question is whether the fourth-order coefficient remains
polynomial after the required signed cancellations: the audited two-entry
estimate controls M^2, whereas a further unresolved clean residence can
produce an M^3 factor that it does not bound.
