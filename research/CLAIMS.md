## Immediate audit findings

1. The draft's acceptance-time noise argument conditions on the stored value and on survival through selection. The claimed probability bound needs a selection-aware proof.
2. The draft invokes exponential drift tails without deriving the required tail statement for its actual process.
3. The draft generalizes the extreme-subproblem potential to interior Tchebycheff subproblems in one paragraph; the V-shaped potential and noise transitions need a complete treatment.
4. Historical coverage in early notebook experiments is weaker than simultaneous coverage in `sim.py`.
5. In `large.py`, `T = 2r+1` describes only unclipped interior neighborhoods. Endpoint neighborhood size is `r+1`, which is especially relevant if endpoints control runtime.
6. Doerr, Elmoutassim, Krejca, and Kibulu (arXiv:2610.35373, 2026) directly analyze communication radius on OneMinMax and prove a different radius dependence for a closely related process. Their quadratic upper bound uses a mutation class with a constant probability of producing a same-Hamming-weight offspring; exact one-bit mutation violates this sufficient condition. Novelty and correctness depend on isolating this and the stopping-event difference.
7. The original whole-process invariant `(J)` is contradicted at initialization. The code's sweep-3 checker tests only an exploratory post-burn-in property.
