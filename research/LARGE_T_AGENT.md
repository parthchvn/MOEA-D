## Check the data, then interpret them

- Verify the campaign packet with the command in [the local guide](LARGE_T_LOCAL.md). Independently compute observed counts, sums, missing counts and means for the two primary arms directly from the receipts. For a first-exact hit at sweep s in an ordinary run, the evaluation cost is (n+1)(1+s). Recompute the primary mean ratio and verify it against `contrasts.csv`; use whole independent trajectories in each arm. Check the two different sample sizes and independent seed sets at n=256 and n=512.
- Inspect all predeclared arms. Give n, T, p=f/n, planned/observed/capped/missing counts, the effect size, direction and interval. For each of U (ideal reference), S−U (time until observed labels align once the reference is ideal), and post−S (subsequent true repair), give the unit and observed denominator. First true exact alignment can precede S. Compare evaluations, recipient comparisons and throughput in their own units. Use `work.csv` for wall time to its recorded endpoint. Check whether any apparent trend comes from early stopping or conditioning on phase completion.
- Use the registered decision rule verbatim. If a required arm is absent, capped, or lacks a confirmed receipt, say which comparison is unavailable and report the restricted summaries or bounds. A missing holdout leaves replication open. Record unfavorable results, ties and possible rare-tail uncertainty. The six fixed-duration throughput cells and supplied-corner trials have distinct purposes.
- For a mechanism, name one observable that would distinguish two explanations. For example, separate a change in S−U from an earlier repair after S, then inspect actual external endpoint repairs. The protocol counts many replacements which leave true weight unchanged; a replacement alone does not demonstrate a useful repair.
